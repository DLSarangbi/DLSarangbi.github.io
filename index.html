The parts worth having

Built for the week you actually have.

The large print handout

One checkbox: 17pt, double spaced. For the people in your pews who can no longer follow small type, and the elders who carry handouts to the homebound.

Search that knows what a passage is

Type "Romans 8" and find every sermon that touched it, down to a verse quoted in passing. "Rom 8:28", "romans 8.28", "Romans 8", all the same.

Your illustrations, findable

Keep the stories you tell, and see where you have told each one. No more repeating the same illustration to the same congregation.

Bring what you already wrote

Import from Word and Google Docs, a whole Takeout folder at once. Ten years in Docs is no reason to stay there.

Any translation you preach from

No Bible ships with SermonDesk, on purpose. Paste a verse from wherever you read it; it is filed with the reference and version. Which translation you preach is your business.

What it does not do yet.

Better read here than found out after paying.

  • Windows only, for now. A Mac version is built and tested on every change, but not yet signed or released. Do not buy this for a Mac yet.
  • In beta. Installers are unsigned, so Windows shows a SmartScreen warning the first time.
  • No phone or tablet version, and none planned. This is a desk tool.
  • No cloud sync of mine. Use Dropbox or OneDrive for that.
  • It checks the subscription when it starts, at most once a day, sending only the licence key. Writing works offline for thirty days; reading and printing, forever.

Terms, refunds, and privacy

The short version, which is also the whole version.

Cancelling, and refunds

Cancel, change a card, or recover a lost key from the billing page linked in your receipt. It signs you in by emailed link. No password, no account to keep.

Cancelling stops the next charge; the subscription runs to the end of the period you have paid for. Cancel on the 2nd and you keep the rest of the month, the last Sunday included.

Refunds are not offered as a matter of course. Try it before you pay: write a real sermon, print the handout, take it to the pulpit, and subscribe only if it is for you. Nothing is charged during the beta.

If something is genuinely broken, or you were charged unexpectedly, email and it will be put right. Where local law gives you a right to a refund, that right stands regardless of anything here.

After a subscription ends the app becomes read only. Every sermon you wrote stays on your computer: openable, searchable, printable, permanently.

Privacy

No account, no password, nothing to sign up for. Your sermons are written to your own disk and never uploaded, read, or transmitted. They are not backed up by us either. That is the other side of the same promise.

The app makes two kinds of network request. One checks whether your licence key is valid, sending only the key, nothing about you or your sermons. The other checks for a new version. No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no tracking.

When you buy, Lemon Squeezy handles your name, email, and payment details under their own privacy policy. We receive your email and what you bought, and use it to send your key and reach you about the software. We do not sell or share it.
