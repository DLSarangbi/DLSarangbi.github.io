Privacy

No account, no password, nothing to sign up for. Your sermons are written to your own disk and never uploaded, read, or transmitted. They are not backed up by us either. That is the other side of the same promise.

The app makes two kinds of network request. One checks whether your licence key is valid, sending only the key, nothing about you or your sermons. The other checks for a new version. No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no tracking. The demo on this page keeps nothing and sends nothing.
